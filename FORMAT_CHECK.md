# Format check for PACER

- Main PDF uses IEEE conference format.
- Body pages are 1--12.
- AI-generated content acknowledgement and references begin on page 13.
- No appendix is included in the main PDF.
- Rendered pages are checked for clipping, overflow, black blocks, and under-filled body page 12.
- LaTeX overfull boxes are rejected by the final submission gate.
- The final package keeps official supplemental material separate from internal review reports.
